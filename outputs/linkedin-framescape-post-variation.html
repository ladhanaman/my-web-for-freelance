Post Variation

I kept thinking about how most gallery sections feel too certain.

Everything is aligned. Everything is explained. Everything is immediately available.

But photographs don’t always live like that in memory.

So I wanted this part of the site to feel softer — like images passing through your attention instead of sitting in rows waiting to be consumed.

The goal wasn’t to make browsing harder. It was to make discovery feel more alive.

Less like a catalog. More like a mood you step into.

And when a collection opens, I wanted it to feel less like navigation and more like continuation — as if the same feeling simply got deeper.

I’m very drawn to this intersection of visual storytelling, interaction design, and photography.

Still shaping it, but this feels close to the kind of digital experience I want to make more of.

Short Voice Script

I didn’t want the photography section to feel too clean or too complete.

I wanted it to feel a little fleeting — like something you notice before you fully understand it.

That’s why the frames move the way they do, and why opening a collection feels more like entering than clicking.

The whole idea was simple:

don’t just display the work — design the feeling around discovering it.

Overlay Lines

  • Not a grid. A feeling.

  • Less browsing. More noticing.
  • Step in. Go deeper.
